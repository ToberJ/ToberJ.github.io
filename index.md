# About

I am an MSCS student at Stanford University, currently working with Prof. <a href="https://profiles.stanford.edu/c-karen-liu">C. Karen Liu</a> at the <a href="https://tml.stanford.edu/">Stanford Movement Lab</a>. Previously, I was an undergraduate student at the University of Washington, where I worked with Prof. <a href="http://www.ranjaykrishna.com/index.html">Ranjay Krishna</a> at <a href="https://raivn.cs.washington.edu/">UW CSE RAIVN Lab</a> and was also involved in research at the <a href="https://allenai.org/">Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)</a>.

My research interests lie in **computer vision** and **embodied AI / robot learning**, with a focus on building perception and decision-making systems that work robustly in the real world. Most recently, I have been working on vision-based reactive whole-body control for humanoid robots.
